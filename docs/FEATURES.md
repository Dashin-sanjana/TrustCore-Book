# Feature inventory and acceptance criteria

All 22 planned modules are scaffolded. **Foundation** means an existing bounded workflow is preserved; it does not mean feature complete. **Scaffold** means contracts/interface/UI marker only, with no operation implementation. Every new interface is proposed pending owner review.

## Scope and status rules

The initial industry is salons/spas. Do not encode this industry into generic appointment, business or service identities. Four initial country/currency defaults exist (LK/LKR, MV/MVR, AE/AED, AU/AUD); market support is not a claim of tax, regulatory or payment-provider readiness.

A module becomes **implemented** only when its documented acceptance criteria, authorization/negative tests, real persistence or configured provider behavior, and review are complete. Never turn a planned stub into a successful mock response. No sales metrics, testimonials, appointments or customers are seeded.
